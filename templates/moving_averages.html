Analysis & Interpretation

Moving Average Analysis:
  • 5-Point Moving Average (Red): Shows short-term trends and smooths out minor fluctuations
  • 1000-Point Moving Average (Teal): Reveals medium-term patterns and reduces noise
  • 5000-Point Moving Average (Yellow): Indicates long-term trends and overall signal direction
  • Original Signal (Blue): Displays raw voltage data with all variations and noise
Key Insights:
  • Shorter moving averages are more responsive to recent changes but more volatile
  • Longer moving averages provide smoother trends but may lag behind actual changes
  • Crossovers between different moving averages can signal trend changes
  • This analysis helps distinguish between noise and genuine signal patterns

Technical Details

MA Windows: 5, 1000, 5000
Calculation: Rolling Mean
Data Processing: Pandas Rolling
